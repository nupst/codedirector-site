CodeDirector

Turn codebases and technical docs into narrated tutorial videos.

What it does

CodeDirector is a Windows desktop application for solo developers and educators. It plans a lesson from a codebase and documentation, replays the steps deterministically, adds AI voice-over, camera moves, a cursor overlay and subtitles, and renders a finished tutorial video.

Publishing

When the creator chooses to, CodeDirector can upload the finished video to their own YouTube and TikTok accounts through the official APIs. The creator signs in to their own account in their browser; CodeDirector never sees a password and stores authorization tokens only on the creator's own computer.
